Tips for playing

Save the updated JSON after each turn and attach it again whenever you begin a new chat or move to a different AI. Long-running games require an explicit save file to maintain continuity. AI providers may differ in how accurately they load large files, apply simulation rules and export updates.

All characters, cultures and geography in Taluma are fictional. The published Taluma save is a snapshot; later official updates do not change a downloaded branch.
