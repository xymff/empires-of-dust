# The Measure of Enough

*Empires of Dust: Taluma · Year 16 (3985 BCE) · Early Spring, Day 231 · 72 days advanced*

Taluma stops mistaking a full storehouse for prosperity, tests Renka's improved frames, and makes accountability practical.

## The abundance problem

By the time warm winds crossed Taluma's drying racks, Nemi had learned that a large store could demand as much judgment as an empty one.

Dava and six rotating apprentices inspected dated baskets. Some bundles remained sound enough to travel. Others entered ordinary meals promptly. Four settlement-days of spoiled or damaged stock were discarded. Aret experimented with raised, cross-ventilated store platforms. Two early lids trapped moisture and had to be dismantled.

At a public hearing, Iset challenged the idea that all incoming food trade was pure profit. If Taluma sent fifteen days of food and received eight days of other food, it had not gained seven days. Maro corrected the accounts to record outgoing and incoming goods separately.

The council adopted a durable food policy: protect a provisional 180 settlement-day floor, rotate inspected stores, support family recovery and nutrition, and commit only verified surplus to voluntary exchanges and voyages.

The important measure was now usable food, not just a large number.

## Renka changes the load

Ilan and Kelan reached Upper Windgrass only after Oru and Tesh obtained permission. Renka's household chose the meeting place and excluded the dangerous loose-stone descent.

Kelan tested a lower load point with a replaceable gripping sleeve. Renka insisted on stone-filled baskets rather than fragile cargo or people. Seven of eight wet-ground trials held; one shifted during a turn. The result was promising, not proof that the design was safe for steep hauling.

Renka's companion demonstrated a woven shoulder support that spread pressure. Leni recorded the movements, leaving private trail symbols off the teaching tablets. The visitors offered inspected food and two cordage coils in an openly counted exchange.

"A path isn't yours because you know where it goes," Kelan told the apprentices later.

## The work of a constitution

Iset challenged a council proposal discussed in the morning while fishers and caregivers could not attend. Maro recorded her objection. At a second hearing, costs became clearer and the council limited the shipment to the amount already accounted for.

Seven councillors still occupied seven seats. Nobody had died, resigned or inherited a position. The new constitution was working through ordinary criticism and corrected numbers.

Meanwhile, Windcut's fishers tried further sample lashings on their own removable frame. A sleeved binding resisted slipping but showed abrasion; another loosened after being retied with wet hands. Vessa refused commercial export until a longer test justified it.
