# The Choice of Seven

*Empires of Dust: Taluma · Year 16 (3985 BCE) · Late Winter, Day 159 · 63 days advanced*

The constitution is completed, food abundance gets a serious audit, and Windcut's fishers hold Taluma to evidence.

## The constitution

At the winter hearings, Maro arranged small clay counters beside seven empty circles, one for each council seat. Esha asked the people to imagine one vacant.

Nemi wanted an interim official capable of keeping the drying racks, trading ground and fishing crews functioning. Seli wanted assurance that a caretaker would not become permanent simply by remaining long enough.

Dava proposed that the six remaining councillors could appoint a willing caretaker for no more than one moon cycle. The caretaker could coordinate ordinary services but could not declare war, sign a treaty, impose compulsory labor or claim new territory.

Any willing adult could be nominated by two resident households. Each adult, including elders, would have one confidential pebble choice, with voting hours accommodating work and care. A majority would elect the successor, with a runoff if necessary. Five independent residents chosen by lot could hear procedural appeals and order a new selection when rules were broken.

After two public readings, the constitution was ratified. Esha moved her pebble out of its circle for a moment. The circle remained.

## The stores that were too full

Nemi faced a different problem: 314 settlement-days of food. It was an extraordinary achievement, but the stores were not one enormous, perfectly preserved supply. An old basket was opened. The upper layer was sound; its interior smelled wrong.

Eight settlement-days of spoiled or rejected food were recorded. Seli would not distribute doubtful food merely because the stores were full.

The council began rotating the oldest sound food first and inspecting stock before exchange. Six additional settlement-days went to voluntary nutrition, recovery and caregiving support. Twelve settlement-days were exchanged or gifted through limited, inspected contacts with willing neighbors. A provisional protected floor of 180 settlement-days was proposed, with existing family provisions and seed reserve untouched.

An apprentice stared at the remaining stores. "What do we do with all the rest?"

"Make sure it remains food," Nemi answered.

## A borrowed frame and a tested knot

Ilan returned Renka's borrowed carrying frame through Oru and Tesh, along the permitted inland approach. At an invited upper-shelf meeting, Kelan demonstrated a textured protective wrapping. Four dry-load pulls performed well. In wet conditions, two of six trials slipped. No dangerous slope test was authorized.

Later, two Ridge Hearth adults attended a voluntary craft meeting in Taluma. No permanent road or treaty resulted.

At Windcut, Vara invited another controlled comparison. Four lashings were fitted to a removable host-owned frame, two conventional and two with Taluman sleeves. After rough tides one ordinary knot slipped and neither sleeved knot did, though one sleeve showed abrasion.

An apprentice called the design ready for export. "We have two tides," Vessa replied. "A season has more." No bulk production began.
