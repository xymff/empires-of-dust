# The Measure of a Promise

*Empires of Dust: Taluma · Year 16 (3985 BCE) · Early Summer, Day 357 · 62 days advanced*

Reliable food, a safer inland load, and a northern opening that may not be a passage. Taluma learns what trust costs.

## The value of a promise

At the Hall of Many Hands, Nemi set a sound smoked-fish packet beside a spoiled one. From the outside they were nearly indistinguishable. Aret loosened the second binding and its damp center answered for them.

"Nobody trades with a basket's tally mark," Nemi said. "They trade with whoever put that mark there."

Dava and six rotating apprentices tracked comparable lots. Ten of twelve batches on improved raised shelves remained sound; on the older covered shelves, eight of twelve were sound. The difference mattered, but Aret would not claim a full season's proof. Wind-driven rain reached some screens and three settlement-days of damaged or questionable food were lost.

Shellbend households accepted an invited exchange. Reedmouth traders inspected dated packets. A Ternhook household received a trial lot at a permitted landing. Fifteen settlement-days of inspected food left Taluma; five days of other provisions returned alongside separately counted materials.

When a visitor asked about automatic monthly deliveries, Maro crossed the word "promised" from an apprentice's notes. "A second invitation is not owed because the first was pleasant."

## Renka changes the question

Ilan waited at Upper Path while Oru and Tesh secured permission from Renka's household. Kelan brought a frame with a lower load point and two replaceable grips. Renka chose gentle wet ground for fourteen trials. Twelve loads stayed secure; two shifted. One carrier suffered a minor bruise, and the group stopped to inspect the assembly.

Renka adjusted the shoulder strap and showed how the load moved in a turn.

"You changed the knot," Ilan told Kelan, following her gestures. "She changed the load."

Later, Renka's companion visited Taluma to teach the combination at the Hall. Leni credited the host teachers and did not record private route signs. No permanent corridor or territorial right followed.

## The opening in the northern rocks

Letha, Rela and Keshu took Reedwing north, using an approved Ternhook stopping place. Beyond Northreach Estuary the crew approached the dark ridge that had haunted earlier voyages.

With a marked sounding line and sufficient provisions to observe a tide cycle, Letha saw a narrow opening between outer rocks. During flood tide, water seemed to continue farther inland. Keshu would not call it a channel.

When the tide reversed, white water boiled over stones that had been hidden. Letha ordered Reedwing away.

Back at Taluma, Maro put two separate marks on the hide atlas: one for the observed notch and another for an unverified possible passage. Nobody landed or claimed the place.

## The public record

Iset questioned the cost of promising deliveries before Taluma knew what the exchanges truly returned. Maro read the food and boat costs aloud. The council agreed each future delivery required fresh consent, verified goods and a separate decision.

Esha listened. All seven councillors still served, but the constitution was no longer an abstraction. People were challenging decisions and improving policy without destabilizing the community.

Nila slept through the argument on Seli's lap. Taluma had survived scarcity; now it was learning the responsibilities of abundance.
