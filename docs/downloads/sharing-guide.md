# Share Empires of Dust with another AI

**Two ways to play**

**Continue Taluma:** Download [the complete Taluma save](../data/taluma.json), attach it to your AI assistant and use [the Taluma continuation instructions](resume-taluma-in-your-ai.txt). This creates an independent branch of Taluma's history. Do not treat the creator's subsequent updates as changes to your own branch unless you choose to import them.

**Start somewhere new:** Paste [the quick-start prompt](quick-start-prompt.txt) into a new conversation. For stronger continuity, also provide [game-master instructions](game-master-instructions.txt). The [new-civilization starter JSON](new-civilization-starter.json) is an example starting save, not a required setting; ask your AI to generate a fresh location and people when you prefer.

**Maintaining save files**

At the end of every advancing turn, ask your AI to return a complete JSON save with the latest date, resources, geography, population, named characters, pending projects, unresolved threads, standing orders and chronicle. Store periodic immutable checkpoints. Always load the last save before resuming a separate chat; AI conversation memory alone may not preserve a large campaign reliably.

**Game rules**

- The player is an immortal guiding intelligence, not a literal ruler.
- Advance time only on an explicit Continue or equivalent direction.
- The council manages routine survival and administration within its delegated authority.
- Reconcile food, fuel, housing and materials; do not invent free resources.
- Keep unknown lands unknown and permission/territory agreements accurate.
- Make progress gradual and outcomes uncertain, but do not stall achievements endlessly.
- Narrate character relationships and consequences, not just a progress log.
- Offer relevant strategic choices and freeform orders.
- Use AI-generated imagery only when requested; attribution/watermark: @thewebbie.
